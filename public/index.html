Your best five cards out of your two plus the five on the board play. Ties are broken by the next-highest cards (the kicker).

Quick lessons
Outs
Cards left in the deck that improve your hand. A flush draw has 9.
Rule of 2 and 4
On the flop, outs × 4 ≈ your % chance to hit by the river. On the turn, outs × 2.
Pot odds
If calling $10 to win a $30 pot, you pay 10 / (30 + 10) = 25% of the final pot, so you need to win more than 25% of the time.
Position
Acting last is an advantage: you see what everyone else does first. Play more hands late, fewer early.
